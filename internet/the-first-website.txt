The World Wide Web was invented by Tim Berners-Lee while he was working at CERN, the European research laboratory near Geneva.

The first website went online in 1991. It lived at info.cern.ch and explained what the web was, how to make your own pages, and how to search for information.

It had no pictures, no videos and no adverts. It was just text and links: words that you could click to jump to other words.

Today there are well over a billion websites. Every one of them still works on the same simple idea: a page, a link, and a click.
