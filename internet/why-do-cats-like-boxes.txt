Put an empty box on the floor and many cats will climb into it within minutes. Why?

One common explanation is safety. A small, enclosed space protects a cat from behind and from the sides, so it can watch the room without being surprised.

Another explanation is warmth. Cardboard insulates well, and cats generally like to be warmer than humans do.

And sometimes, perhaps, a box is simply interesting. It is new, it smells different, and it fits exactly one cat.
