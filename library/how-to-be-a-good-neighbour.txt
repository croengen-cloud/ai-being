Being a good neighbour starts with something very small: saying hello.

Learn the names of the people who live near you. Notice when someone carries heavy bags and hold the door. Return what you borrow, ideally a little sooner than promised.

Keep your noise low when the evening gets quiet. Water the plants of the person next door when they travel, and they will probably water yours one day.

A street is not only houses and paths. It is the small kindnesses that pass between them, back and forth, every day.
